THE DETAILS / 08

A few good questions.

Have something more specific? Bring it to a demo.

Talk to the team →
Who is this for?+

Mid-market and private equity-backed organizations with lean IT and security teams, including the CFOs, CIOs, CTOs, and IT leaders responsible for security, risk, and compliance decisions.

Does it replace existing security tools?+

No. The platform offers more than 50 integrations using your own API keys. Confirm which of your existing tools and workflows are supported in the Advantek configuration you are evaluating.

Do we need a dedicated security team to use it?+

No. Guided workflows, expert personas, and expert-written prompts give IT generalists a structured way to draft a policy review or threat assessment. Your team validates the findings and owns the decision.

Can it help with GRC or vulnerabilities?+

Yes. Teams use it to run gap analyses and policy updates against frameworks such as HIPAA, CMMC 2.0, ISO 27001, NIST, and CIS Controls, and to prioritize vulnerabilities by real-world exposure. A demo can show the available capabilities and what your team would need to validate.

How is our data handled?+

Ask Advantek how your prompts and outputs are stored, who can access them, and what model-training terms apply to your configuration. Review data ownership, retention, and contractual safeguards as part of your evaluation.

What happens during a demo?+

We'll discuss your priorities, walk through a relevant product experience, outline the potential cost and time savings for your team, and identify the next steps for your environment.

What kind of return should we expect?+

It depends on how much security and compliance work you currently outsource, defer, or would need to hire for. During a demo, we'll estimate the return using your own workload, including consulting spend, staff time, and audit preparation.

How does pricing work?+

Ask Advantek for an annual subscription quote based on your requirements and deployment. The proposal should identify the included workflows, any usage charges, and optional services.
